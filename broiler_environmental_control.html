01

The OR Gate Logic

The OR gate (U1A) evaluates two key environmental threats: excess heat load (A) or toxic ammonia/carbon-dioxide accumulation (B). If **either** condition returns a logic high signal (1), the output triggers the primary tunnel ventilation request.

Formula: YOR=A∨B
02

The Cold-Draft Interlock

The primary AND gate (U2A) checks if ventilation is requested (A∨B) **AND** if ambient temperature is above the chick-brooding draft threshold (CSAFE). This guarantees high-velocity tunnel fans never blow chilling wind on young broilers during cold hours.

Formula: YFAN=(A∨B)∧C
03

Evaporative Fogging Lockout

The secondary AND gate (U3A) controls cooling foggers (YFOG). Foggers require active airflow (YFAN) **AND** true high thermal stress (A). Gas-only purges (B) never trigger foggers, preventing wet litter and ascites risks.

Formula: YFOG=YFAN∧A
