2. Executive Summary

Lwandile Engineering Projects, in strategic partnership with the Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy (founded by Dr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure), formally submits this Expression of Interest (EOI) under Window 4 of the Just Energy Transition (JET) Funding Platform. Recognizing that JET funding strictly evaluates registered, high-capacity institutional partnerships rather than standalone entities, our consortium delivers an end-to-end framework combining rigorous engineering execution with accredited institutional governance and long-term skills transfer.

As coal-fired power infrastructure transitions out of service across Mpumalanga, vulnerable community ecosystems face critical economic challenges. Our consortium deploys an integrated bio-solar and circular agrifood model combining agrivoltaics and controlled-environment hydroponics with an organic waste-to-value biogas and composting pipeline. The immediate rollout is anchored by a R3.73M Scaled MVP Scope secured inside a 20,000m² Perimeter Security Fence.

The operational roadmap is engineered across a 12-Month Buffered MVP Execution Schedule (5,640 Labor Man-Hours & 530 Plant-Hours) culminating in a 36-Month Expansion Horizon that scales the campus into a full 2.5MW Agrivoltaics Clean Energy & Agrifood Hub. Executed under the Scapush Precision Execution Standard, this alliance guarantees institutional integrity, SARS compliance, and scalable socioeconomic impact.

4. Key Focus Areas & Technical Scope

4.1 Climate-Smart Agriculture & Agrivoltaics Scaling (30 kWp → 2.5 MW)
  • Agrivoltaic Dual-Use Infrastructure: Immediate deployment of 30 kWp solar PV with 60 kWh LFP BESS, serving as the foundational module for the 36-month expansion into a 2.5MW Agrivoltaics solar canopy providing shading and microclimate control.
  • Sovereign Bio-Solar Barns: Barn 1 (Poultry - 4,800 broilers) and Barn 2 (Swine - 160 head) integrated with 2% sloped gravity flumes feeding the anaerobic digester.
  • Controlled-Environment Agriculture: Zone F Nursery equipped with hydronic underfloor heating loops powered by biogas CHP thermal recovery.
4.2 Circular Bioeconomy & Waste-to-Value (70 m³ Digester & 15 kW CHP)
  • Anaerobic Digestion: Construction of a 70m³ fixed-dome masonry biodigester converting swine slurry and agricultural waste into continuous biogas.
  • Thermal & Electrical Co-Generation: 15 kW Biogas CHP generator providing off-grid baseload power and hot water loop heat for the nursery and brooding pads.
  • Organic Fertilizer & Composting: Nutrient-rich digestate processing for soil rehabilitation across surrounding local outgrower farms.
4.3 Governance, Skills Transfer & Accessibility Hub
  • Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy: Accredited training modules led by Dr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure, housed in a dedicated 12m container classroom.
  • Zone M Accessibility Hub: Step-free universal access paving, 4x wheelchair bays, and a 30kW DC EV charger powered by the microgrid.
  • SCADA Telemetry & Control: 120-tag PLC control center providing real-time data tracking of power, thermal, and biosecurity metrics.
