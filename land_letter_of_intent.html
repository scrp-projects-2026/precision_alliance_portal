1. Purpose & Executive Statement of Intent / Inhloso Yesivumelwano

English Version [EN]

This Letter of Intent (LOI) confirms the mutual agreement between Lwandile Engineering Projects (Pty) Ltd (led by Sipho Wiseman Khuzwayo) in partnership with the Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy (led by Dr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure) and the Phakamani Primary Disability Enterprise Co-op (represented by community leaders Mduduzi Wiseman Nkosi, S'fiso Elphuas Shongwe, and Manese Emaly Qaba as Disability Woman Representative).

Inguqulo yesiZulu [ZU]

Le ncwadi yenhloso (LOI) iqinisekisa ukuvunyelwa ngokubambisana phakathi kwe-Lwandile Engineering Projects (eholwa uSipho Wiseman Khuzwayo) ngokubambisana ne-Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy (eholwa uDr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure) kanye ne-Phakamani Primary Disability Enterprise Co-op (emelwe abaholi bomphakathi uMduduzi Wiseman Nkosi, uS'fiso Elphuas Shongwe, noManese Emaly Qaba njengoMme Omele Abantu Abaphila Nokukhubazeka).

THE IRRESISTABLE PARTNERSHIP PROPOSITION / ISIPHAKAMISO SOKUBAMBISANA

This agreement guarantees that Stand 279 Grootspruit-Embalenhle will anchor the Sovereign Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus, empowering persons with disabilities through community equity participation, accessible infrastructure, electric wheelchair charging hubs, and priority access to green-technology training, employment, and enterprise incubation. Solar and biogas will support energy and heat generation, alongside piggery and poultry facilities, fostering inclusive, sustainable local economic development and skills transfer under the Phakamani Primary Disability Enterprise Co-op.

Lesi sivumelwano siqinisekisa ukuthi isiza esingu-Stand 279 Grootspruit-Embalenhle sizosetshenziselwa ukusungula iSikhungo se-Sovereign Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus. Lesisivumelwano siqinisekisa ukuthuthukiswa kwamakhono, namathuba emisebenzi ikakhulukazi emphakathini ophila ngokukhubazeka ngaphansi kwe-Phakamani Primary Disability Enterprise Co-op. Leprojhekthi izoba namahhoko amabili okukhulisa izingulube kanye nezinkukhu, futhi ibe neziteshi zokushaja izihlalo ezinamasondo zikagesi, ugesi nokushisa siyaziphehlela ngokusebenzisa i-solar ngokubambisana ne-Biogas.

2. Key Partnership Terms & Land Allocation / Imigomo Ebalulekile Yokubambisana

Partnership Term (EN) / Umgomo (ZU) Specific Commitment & Technical Detail Beneficiary Impact / Umthelela Kumphakathi
Land Stand Reference Stand 279 Grootspruit-Embalenhle allocated for campus co-development. Secures prime accredited site for Phase 1 MVP deployment under community stewardship.
Ownership & Lease Model Long-term 20-Year Renewable Leasehold with a structured Community Dividend Share. Sustainable, recurring rental and enterprise revenue for local disability cooperative funds.
Mobility Infrastructure Construction of a fully accessible Accessibility Hub including 4× Wheelchair Charging Bays & 30 kW DC EV Charger. Free, 100% off-grid solar charging for electric wheelchairs and mobility scooters.
Preferential Employment & Training Minimum 30% direct placement quota for persons with disabilities in SCADA monitoring, administration, and campus operations. Direct human capital development via the Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy.
JET EOI Strategic Weight Full inclusion of Phakamani Primary Disability Enterprise Co-op leadership within the official Just Energy Transition funding submission. High-priority scoring for social inclusion and socio-economic transformation.

3. Mutual Obligations of the Parties / Izibopho Ezihlanganyelwe

3.1 Obligations of Lwandile Engineering & Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy
  • Full capital raising and technical execution under the Scapush Precision Execution Standard.
  • Construction of 100% step-free, universally accessible pathways across the campus footprint at Stand 279 Grootspruit-Embalenhle.
  • Provision of accredited green skills training modules (Solar, SCADA, Livestock, Biogas) tailored for persons with disabilities.
  • Installation of clean water points and zero-diesel solar power hubs for local community benefit.
3.2 Obligations of Phakamani Primary Disability Enterprise Co-op
  • Guaranteeing undisputed land availability and support for site access at Stand 279 Grootspruit-Embalenhle during environmental and civil works.
  • Active participation in the Campus Governance Board to oversee community integration and equity distribution.
  • Mobilizing community candidates for operator certification and green enterprise incubation programs.
